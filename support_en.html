FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app, go to Profile, then tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID you used for the original purchase.

Is Premium a subscription?

No. Premium is a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase.

What is free?

Basic dictionary lookup, inflection lookup, IPA, audio, saved words, and reading features are available for free. Premium unlocks advanced dictionary content, practice modes, enhanced review, and unlimited vocabulary features.

Does the app need internet?

The core dictionary and readings are bundled with the app and work offline. StoreKit purchase and restore actions require Apple's App Store connection.

Which devices are supported?

Wello Italiano English supports iOS 16 and later.

How is my learning data stored?

Your saved words, progress, calendar, and settings are stored locally on your device. No account is required.
