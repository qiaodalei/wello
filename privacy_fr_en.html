Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 27, 2026

Wello French does not require an account, does not collect personal data, does not track users, and does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Your saved words, progress, settings, and study records are stored locally on your device. Deleting the app removes this local data.

In-app purchases are processed by Apple StoreKit. We do not receive your payment card number or Apple ID password.

For support, please use the contact information on the support page.
