FAQ

Does the app require internet? Dictionary and reading content are available offline. In-app purchase and Restore Purchases require App Store access.

How do I restore a purchase? Open Profile and tap Restore Purchases. Use the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

Is Premium a subscription? No. Premium is a one-time non-consumable in-app purchase.

Where is my learning data stored? Saved words, progress, settings, and study records are stored locally on your device.
