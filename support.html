常见问题

如何恢复购买？

打开 App →「我的」→ 升级卡片下方点击「恢复购买」，用购买时的同一个 Apple ID 即可一键还原已解锁的高级版权益，无需再次付费。

换了新手机，高级版还在吗？

在。用同一 Apple ID 登录后，进入 App 点「恢复购买」即可。一次性内购的权益与你的 Apple 账户绑定。

免费能用到什么程度？

查词、变形反查、基础释义/音标/发音、全部级别的长文阅读都永久免费。词条的进阶内容（完整变位表、词形变化、搭配、近义辨析、更多例句）前 50 个词免费体验，之后基础信息仍免费，进阶内容一次性付费解锁全部。

需要联网吗？会用流量吗？

不需要。词库与文章全部内置，完全离线使用，不消耗流量。

如何清空我的学习数据？

打开 App →「我的」→「设置」→ 数据与隐私 →「清空学习数据」，按提示确认即可。该操作不可恢复。

支持哪些 iPhone / 系统版本？

支持 iOS 16 及以上系统（覆盖 iPhone 12 及更新机型）。

FAQ

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the app → "Profile" → tap "Restore Purchases" under the upgrade card. Sign in with the same Apple ID you used to buy, and your premium access is restored at no extra cost.

I got a new iPhone — is premium still mine?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple ID and tap "Restore Purchases". The one-time purchase is tied to your Apple account.

What's free?

Dictionary lookup, inflection reverse-lookup, basic definitions/IPA/audio, and graded reading at all levels are free forever. Advanced entry content (full conjugations, morphology, collocations, synonyms, more examples) is free for your first 50 words, then unlocked for all words with a one-time purchase.

Does it need internet?

No. The dictionary and articles are bundled and work fully offline.

How do I clear my data?

Open the app → "Profile" → "Settings" → Data & Privacy → "Clear learning data".

Which devices are supported?

iOS 16 and later (iPhone 12 and newer).
